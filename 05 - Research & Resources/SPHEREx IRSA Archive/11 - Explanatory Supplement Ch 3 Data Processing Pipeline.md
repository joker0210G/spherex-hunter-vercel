## 🔄 3.1 Raw Data Ingestion & Sample-Up-the-Ramp (SUR)
SPHEREx detectors are clocked continuously in non-destructive **Sample-Up-the-Ramp (SUR)** mode without mechanical shutters:
1. **Frame Readout Time:** $T_{\text{fr}} = 1.5349\text{ s}$ per full-frame read.
2. **Onboard Slope Fitting:** The custom Video8 ASICs compute a real-time linear least-squares regression of signal charge accumulation versus time across $N$ reads.
3. **Telemetry Packets:** Level 1 products transmit the fitted slope (photocurrent in $\text{e}^-/\text{s}$), the sample count $N$, and hardware transient flags (`TRANSIENT`, `OVERFLOW`, `SUR_ERROR`).

## 🛠️ 3.2 The 12-Step Level 2 Science Processing Sequence

```
Level 1 SUR Downlink (e-/s, N, SUR Flags)
 │
 ├── 1. Blooming, Snowball & Transient Halo Flagging (Sect. 3.2.1)
 ├── 2. Nonlinearity Correction: 5th-order polynomial inverse (Sect. 3.2.2)
 ├── 3. Variance Map: Robberto (2007) read + Poisson noise (Sect. 3.2.3)
 ├── 4. Persistence Masking: Multi-exponential decay modeling (Sect. 3.2.4)
 ├── 5. Bad Pixel Flagging: Table 16 22-bit mask assignment (Sect. 3.2.5)
 ├── 6. Dark Subtraction: 2D cryogenic thermal dark maps (Sect. 3.2.6)
 ├── 7. Absolute Gain Correction: Conversion to MJy/sr (Sect. 3.2.7)
 ├── 8. Fine Astrometry: Gaia DR3 cross-match & SIP distortion (Sect. 3.2.8)
 ├── 9. Source Masking: Bright star radii & ghost masking (Sect. 3.2.9)
 ├── 10. Outlier Pixel Detection: Multi-pointing rejection (Sect. 3.2.10)
 ├── 11. Zodiacal Background Prediction: Kelsall (1998) model (Sect. 3.2.11)
 └── 12. Level 2 Data Quality Assessment (L2DQA) (Sect. 3.2.12)
```

---

### 1. Blooming, Snowball & Halo Flagging (Sect. 3.2.1)
- **Source Blooming:** Over-saturated pixels spill charge along detector columns. Flagged as `CORE_BLOOM` (center) and `MANTLE_BLOOM` (edges), stamped with Bit 26 (`BLOOM`).
- **Snowballs:** Enormous charge clusters ($> 100,000\ \text{e}^-$) generated by high-energy primary cosmic rays impacting the substrate. Saturated core is masked as Bit 27 (`SNOWBALL`) and outer halo as Bit 28 (`HALO`).

### 2. Nonlinearity Correction (Sect. 3.2.2)
Near well saturation, charge collection efficiency drops. The pipeline linearizes the accumulating signal using laboratory calibration parameters ($Q_{\text{nl}}, b_1, b_2, b_3, Q_{\text{max}}$). If a pixel exceeds the linear regime or required correction $> 50\%$, Bit 15 (`NONLINEAR`) is set.

### 3. Empirical Variance Mapping (Sect. 3.2.3)
The per-pixel flux variance is computed by combining measured laboratory read noise $\sigma_{\text{det}}^2(i, j, N)$ with the Poisson shot noise equation from Robberto (2007):
$$\text{Var}(F) = \frac{1}{T_{\text{int}}(N)^2} \left[ \sigma_{\text{det}}^2(i, j, N) + \frac{6}{5}\frac{N^2+1}{N^2-1} F \cdot T_{\text{int}}(N) \right]$$
- $F$: Measured slope flux ($\text{e}^-/\text{s}$).
- $N$: Number of valid non-destructive reads in the ramp.
- $T_{\text{int}}(N)$: Integration duration across $N$ reads.
- Stored as the 2D floating-point `VARIANCE` HDU in $(\text{MJy/sr})^2$.

### 4. Multi-Exponential Persistence Modeling (Sect. 3.2.4)
Residual trapped charge from bright sources in previous exposures decays slowly into subsequent pointings:
$$I_p(t) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} A_n C_n e^{-t/\tau_n}$$
- $t$: Elapsed time since completion of prior exposure.
- $\tau_n$: Characteristic trap release time constants.
- $F = f \times t_{\text{int}}$: Saturated fluence of prior target.
- Pixels with residual persistence current exceeding the local noise threshold are flagged with Bit 17 (`PERSIST`) to prevent false-positive transient detection.

### 5. Fine Astrometry & SIP Distortion (Sect. 3.2.8)
- Stellar positions from **Gaia DR3** are propagated to the exact observation epoch using trigonometric proper motions ($\mu_\alpha \cos \delta, \mu_\delta$).
- Derives refined FITS WCS keywords and Simple Imaging Polynomial (SIP) distortion terms.
- **Quality Indicator:** The header keyword `FINAST` is assigned:
  - `FINAST = 0`: Solution converged with residual RMS astrometric error $\le 0.1''$.
  - `FINAST > 0`: Degraded or unconstrained astrometry solution.

### 6. Bright Source Masking Radii (Sect. 3.2.9)
Diffraction spikes, optical halos, and frame edge ghosts from bright sources are masked according to empirical scaling relations (Table 19 & 20):
- **Frame Edge Ghost Dimensions:**
  $$\text{Length} = \left\lceil 11 \cdot \left( 25,000 \cdot 10^{-m_{\text{AB}}/2.5} + 24 \right) \right\rceil\text{ pixels}$$
  $$\text{Width} = 2 \left\lceil 4 \cdot \left( 25,000 \cdot 10^{-m_{\text{AB}}/2.5} + 1 \right) \right\rceil\text{ pixels}$$
- **Faint Limit:** Sources fainter than $19\text{ AB mag}$ are not masked by default.

### 7. Level 2 Data Quality Assessment (L2DQA, Sect. 3.2.12)
An exposure is certified with `L2DQA = PASS` only if:
1. **Nonlinearity:** No valid pixel in the science image has a flux correction exceeding $50\%$.
2. **Variance:** The measured per-pixel variance matches the theoretical noise model within tolerances.
3. **Astrometry:** `FINAST == 0` (astrometric convergence achieved).

## 🎯 3.3 Photometric Measurements (Forced Aperture Photometry)
The Level 3 forced photometry pipeline extracts calibrated flux densities from Level 2 MEF images:
- **Prior Catalog Input:** Target coordinates from Gaia DR3, 2MASS, and AllWISE.
- **Pixel Rejection Bitmask:** Pixels are rigorously excluded from flux aperture sums if any of the following flags are active:
  ```
  TRANSIENT (0x0001) | OVERFLOW (0x0002) | SUR_ERROR (0x0004) | NONFUNC (0x0040) |
  DICHROIC (0x0080)  | MISSING_DATA (0x0200) | OUTLIER (0x00080000)
  ```
- **ePSF Profile Fitting:** Convolves the zone-specific effective Point Spread Function (`ePSF`) to determine point-source flux density and formal uncertainty.

## 🗺️ 3.4 Mosaic Generation & Spatial Reprojection
The SPHEREx Mosaic Tool builds custom spatial-spectral co-adds:
1. **Data Pre-Filtering:** Excludes exposures failing `L2DQA` or corrupted by severe satellite streaks.
2. **Spectral Channel Binning:** Grouped into 102 discrete wavelength channels matching the nominal LVF dispersion.
3. **Area-Overlap Reprojection:** Resamples Level 2 pixels onto a user-defined tangent plane grid, weighting input pixels by inverse variance ($w_i = 1/\sigma_i^2$).
4. **Outputs:** Produces multi-plane FITS mosaics with flux, total coverage depth (`NHITS`), and propagated error planes.
