## Comparison of Available Archival Surveys

| Survey | Wavelengths | Pixel Resolution | Epochs | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SPHEREx** | $0.75 - 5.0\ \mu	ext{m}$ (102 bands) | $6.2	ext{ arcsec}$ | 2025–2027 (Every 6 months) | **Primary Target:** Molecular ice spectroscopy, thermal IR transients. |
| **WISE / NEOWISE**| $3.4, 4.6, 12, 22\ \mu	ext{m}$ | $6.1	ext{ arcsec}$ | 2010–2024 (Multi-year) | **Historical Baseline:** Verifying 15-year proper motion of brown dwarfs. |
| **Gaia DR3** | Optical ($0.33 - 1.05\ \mu	ext{m}$) | $0.05 - 0.4	ext{ arcsec}$ | 2014–2022 | **Astrometry:** Highly accurate stellar positions and proper motion vectors. |
| **2MASS** | $1.25\ (J), 1.65\ (H), 2.17\ (Ks)\ \mu	ext{m}$ | $2.0	ext{ arcsec}$ | 1997–2001 | **Deep Baseline:** 25+ year time baseline to confirm high proper motion stars. |
