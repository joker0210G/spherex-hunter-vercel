# 🎨 Role: UI-UX & Citizen Science Lead
> **Mission:** Design an intuitive, NASA-grade citizen science experience and lead the final video and pitch presentation.

---

## 🎯 Key Deliverables
1. **User Interface & Design System:**
   - Dark theme inspired by NASA Mission Control and James Webb / SPHEREx dashboards.
   - Clean HUD (Heads-Up Display) overlay showing: Target Name, RA, Dec, FOV, Epoch Date, Bandpass, and Motion Speed.
   - Citizen science feedback loop: **"Flag Candidate"** button allowing users to submit their discovery coordinates to a public community list.
2. **Interactive Walkthrough / Onboarding:**
   - Quick 3-step tutorial:
     1. Pick a target or enter coordinates.
     2. Watch the blink animation or toggle difference imaging.
     3. Identify moving dipoles or variable flaring!
3. **Pitch Deck & Demo Video Lead:**
   - Script and record the **30-second to 2-minute demo video** (mandatory for Space Apps).
   - Produce the 7-slide pitch presentation following the story structure in [[Winning Project Pitch Deck Outline]].

---

## 💡 Citizen Science Inspiration: Backyard Worlds
Look at [Backyard Worlds: Planet 9](https://www.zooniverse.org/projects/marckuchner/backyard-worlds-planet-9):
- Why it works: Simplicity! Volunteers don't need astrophysics PhDs; human vision is naturally evolved to detect movement in sequential frames (blink comparator principle, the exact way Clyde Tombaugh found Pluto in 1930).
- Our innovation: We bring this same power to **SPHEREx's 102 infrared bands**, with instant on-the-fly spectra!
