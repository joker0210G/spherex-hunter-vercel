NASA SPHEREx Space Observatory (2025-2028) 0.75 – 5.0 μm

SPHEREx performs an all-sky near-infrared spectral survey across 102 individual spectrophotometric bands. Equipped with 6 detector arrays, SPHEREx detects frozen water ice ($H_2O$), carbon dioxide ice ($CO_2$), and methane ($CH_4$) in interstellar clouds and maps Planet X / Brown Dwarf candidates.

NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA) & 2MASS TAP / FinderChart

Hosted at Caltech/JPL, NASA IRSA serves the historical Two Micron All-Sky Survey (2MASS, 1997–2001) in $J\ (1.25\,\mu ext{m})$, $H\ (1.65\,\mu ext{m})$, and $K_s\ (2.17\,\mu ext{m})$ bands. By subtracting 2MASS from SPHEREx across a ~26.2-year temporal baseline, sub-pixel dipoles reveal slow-moving brown dwarfs and trans-Neptunian objects.

Canadian Space Agency NEOSSat & CADC Asteroid Tracking

The Near-Earth Object Surveillance Satellite (NEOSSat) is the world's first space telescope dedicated to detecting and tracking asteroids and satellites. The Canadian Astronomy Data Centre (CADC) provides open access to NEOSSat public archives.

SPHEREx 102-Band Infrared Science Guide

6 Linear Variable Filter (LVF) Detector Arrays × 17 Narrow Spectral Channels

Why 102 Narrow Spectral Bands Matter for Planet X & Cold Brown Dwarfs

Cold sub-stellar bodies like WISE 0855 (at ~250 K / -23°C) and the hypothetical Planet Nine emit almost entirely in the thermal infrared and are completely invisible in optical surveys (Gaia, DSS2). SPHEREx divides the 0.75 µm – 5.00 µm spectrum into 102 contiguous narrow bands, pinpointing the peak thermal emission while distinguishing true cold worlds from background stars.

The 6 Detector Arrays (A1 – A6)

Array 1 (Bands 1–17): 0.750 – 1.110 µm
Optical/NIR boundary, stellar continuum, proper motion validation.
Array 2 (Bands 18–34): 1.110 – 1.640 µm
2MASS J/H-band overlap, warm stellar photospheres, H₂O vapor dip.
Array 3 (Bands 35–51): 1.640 – 2.420 µm
2MASS Ks overlap, circumstellar dust, stellar CO bandheads.
Array 4 (Bands 52–68): 2.420 – 3.820 µm 🔬
H₂O Ice (3.05 µm) and CH₄ Methane (3.30 µm) absorption dips.
Array 5 (Bands 69–85): 3.820 – 4.420 µm 🔬
CO₂ Ice (4.27 µm) and cold Y-dwarf thermal peak.
Array 6 (Bands 86–102): 4.420 – 5.000 µm 🔬
CO Monoxide (4.67 µm), complex organics, and Planet X emission.
