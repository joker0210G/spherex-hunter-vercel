## 💡 Production & Recording Checklist
1. **Pacing:** Practice reading the script at approximately 65–70 words. Total spoken word count above: **68 words** (crisp, clear, 28.5 seconds at natural pace).
2. **Audio Quality:** Clear vocal track, normalized to -14 LUFS, with subtle low-volume synth background music.
3. **Subtitles:** Burn in crisp English subtitles (e.g. yellow or white with black outline) so judges can review silently or in noisy environments.
4. **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) 60 FPS recording of the web interface.
5. **No Persons Under 18:** Ensure no minors appear in video or audio per NASA Space Apps rules.
6. **No NASA Logo Modification:** Use only approved project branding and standard attribution.
